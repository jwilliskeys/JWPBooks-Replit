---
name: Appointment service snapshots
description: Preserve appointment-specific service overrides without changing the reusable master service catalog.
---

Appointment service lines are snapshots of master service entries. Editing a booked service's wording, price, duration, or flags must not change the master catalog entry.

**Why:** Users need to customize services for one appointment while keeping the master service list reusable for future appointments.

**How to apply:** Copy catalog values into appointment-owned service lines, and only edit master defaults from an explicit service-catalog management flow.

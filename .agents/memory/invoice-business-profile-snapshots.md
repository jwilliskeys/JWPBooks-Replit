---
name: Invoice business-profile snapshots
description: How Company Profile edits affect invoice business details.
---

Company business details are captured on new invoices. Updating the Company Profile must not alter the seller name, contact details, or address on existing invoices.

**Why:** The user chose to apply the updated business address to new invoices only.

**How to apply:** When changing invoice creation or invoice rendering, use the stored seller snapshot for an invoice and read the current profile only for new invoices or unsaved invoice drafts.
